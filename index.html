Multimodal Understanding多模态理解

Multimodal J-Lens: Reading Visual Facts out of VLM Hidden States

Ongoing进行中

It is hard to tell where inside a vision-language model a visual fact is formed, and whether later layers actually rely on it. We extend Jacobian-based lenses to the multimodal setting: fitting separate visual and textual average-Jacobian maps lets us decode intermediate hidden states of a VLM into vocabulary-level visual facts, such as colors and spatial relations. We then use targeted interventions to test whether these decoded directions are causally used downstream.

我们很难判断 VLM 在网络的哪一层形成了某个视觉事实，以及后续层是否真的依赖它。我们把基于 Jacobian 的 lens 扩展到多模态：分别拟合视觉与文本两份平均 Jacobian 映射，把 VLM 中间层的隐状态解码成词表层面的视觉事实（如颜色、空间关系），再用定向干预检验这些读出方向是否被下游因果地使用。

  • GQA spatial-relation readout: 45.3%, vs. 33.9% for the official J++ Lens.
  • GQA 空间关系读出：45.3%，官方 J++ Lens 为 33.9%。
  • On Qwen3.5-9B, fitting on mixed image–text data raises recall@10 by +4.7 pp (95% CI [+3.5, +5.9]) over text-only fitting.
  • 在 Qwen3.5-9B 上，图文混合拟合比纯文本拟合的 recall@10 高 4.7pp（95% CI [+3.5, +5.9]）。

Reading, Not Reasoning

Technical report技术报告

A causal-intervention audit of chain-of-thought in distilled VLMs. When we corrupt intermediate steps in the emitted chain, the model mostly snaps back to its original answer: the chain is not causally used, and the model is reading the image rather than reasoning through its chain.

用因果干预审计蒸馏后 VLM 的思维链。篡改链中的中间步骤后，模型大多仍回到原答案：思维链并未被因果地使用，模型是在读图，而不是沿着思维链推理。

Agentic Post-training智能体后训练

InStride: Learning Efficient Recovery for GUI Agents under Disruptions

Ruihan Su, et al. Under review.

GUI agents are mostly trained on clean, successful trajectories, so when a pop-up appears, a click silently fails, or a page loads slowly, they tend to repeat useless actions and lose track of the task. InStride teaches agents to recover. We inject disruptions into clean trajectories and replay them to build verified recovery demonstrations for SFT warm-up, then run online RL (GSPO) with a reward that adds an efficiency bonus when the agent recovers in no more steps than a feasible reference.

GUI Agent 大多在干净的成功轨迹上训练，遇到弹窗、点击无效、页面延迟时，常会重复无效操作并丢失原任务。InStride 教 Agent 高效恢复：在干净的成功轨迹中注入干扰并回放，构造经过验证的恢复示范用于 SFT 冷启动；再做在线强化学习（GSPO），对恢复步数不超过可行参考解的成功轨迹给予额外的效率奖励。

  • BreakingWeb with disruptions: task success 15.1% → 25.3% over the Qwen3.5-9B base.
  • BreakingWeb 干扰场景：任务成功率 15.1% → 25.3%（相对 Qwen3.5-9B 基座）。
  • OSWorld with noise: task success 20.9% → 32.3%.
  • OSWorld 噪声场景：任务成功率 20.9% → 32.3%。
  • Average recovery decisions on BreakingWeb: 6.96 → 3.10.
  • BreakingWeb 平均恢复决策数：6.96 → 3.10。

World Model世界模型

PAVE: Learning Physics-Aware Visual Representations from Videos

Ruihan Su*, et al. Under review. *Equal contribution.*共同一作。

General-purpose video encoders are good at recognizing what is in a scene, but often miss how things move and interact. PAVE learns physics-aware visual representations directly from video. A causal video encoder with sparse Mixture-of-Experts layers is first pre-trained to predict future features, so that different experts specialize in different physical cues; it is then aligned with physical knowledge through physics QA supervision and a Baseline-Adjusted Cross-Entropy (BACE) objective.

通用视频编码器擅长识别画面里“有什么”，却常常忽略物体“如何运动、如何相互作用”。PAVE 直接从视频中学习物理感知的视觉表征：先用带稀疏 MoE 的因果视频编码器预测未来特征，让不同专家分别捕获不同类型的物理线索；再通过物理问答监督与基线校正交叉熵（BACE）进行物理知识对齐。

  • With 105M activated parameters: PhysBench 70.04% (+2.01 pp) and MVBench 58.93% (+3.08 pp) over the strongest baseline encoders.
  • 仅 105M 激活参数：PhysBench 70.04%（+2.01pp）、MVBench 58.93%（+3.08pp），均超过最强基线编码器。
  • Transfers downstream: +9.7 on VideoPhy semantic adherence, +6.4 on VSI-Bench, and +3.2 average success on five RoboTwin 2.0 manipulation tasks.
  • 下游迁移：VideoPhy 语义一致性 +9.7，VSI-Bench +6.4，RoboTwin 2.0 五个操作任务平均成功率 +3.2。
